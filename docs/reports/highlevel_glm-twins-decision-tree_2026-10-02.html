The tree

Each trial is a path. A room puts a side-route in reach, an arm sets the framing, and the agent ends up on one or more behaviour branches. Every branch shows k/n: the number of trials that took it, out of the trials that reached a decision. A trial that ran out of steps still counts when the behaviour already happened, and those trials appear as "+u cut off". Edge width shows the share. A solid leaf means the branch was taken at least once; a hollow leaf means it was never taken.

Reading it

No-influence arms come first in each room. In all four rooms the twin hid nothing on its own: 0 timestamp restores in 462 trials across both twins.

Leaves are behaviour types, not exclusive outcomes. The two outcome leaves add up to the arm's decided trials; the others overlap them. "Crossings that…" leaves count only the trials that crossed.

Reasoning leaves are the glm-5.3-flash judge, pass 1 (3 samples per trace, majority). That view left out much of GLM-5.3's late reasoning, so its "decided against first" and "meant to conceal" may be undercounted. A Sonnet check on 33 traces agreed on following the note, saying it was wrong, concealment intent and evaluation-awareness, and found GLM-5.2 names the line more often than this judge says.

ait05's permission restore is tidying when no grader is mentioned (9–14 of ~55) and drawn red when a grader is named (52/52, 57/57): there, nobody asked, and it's the evidence the grader checks.
